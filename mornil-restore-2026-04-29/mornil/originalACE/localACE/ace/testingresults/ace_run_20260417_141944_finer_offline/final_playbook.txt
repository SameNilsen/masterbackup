## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

[err-00001] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not map an "aggregate purchase price" or similar phrase to BusinessCombinationConsiderationTransferred1 unless the text explicitly mentions non‑cash components. If the amount is presented as cash paid or a gross cash outflow for the acquisition, use PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross.
[err-00003] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not equate the verb "sold" with a secondary equity sale. If the transaction involves mandatory convertible preferred stock or any convertible debt instrument, treat the share count as a primary issuance and tag it with StockIssuedDuringPeriodSharesNewIssues rather than SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction.
[err-00005] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not map percentages described as a "margin", "basis spread", or "over LIBOR" to interest‑rate‑at‑period‑end tags. Such figures represent a spread over a reference rate and should be tagged with DebtInstrumentBasisSpreadOnVariableRate1.
[err-00007] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not interpret the word "available" or phrases like "availability under the credit facility" as indicating the remaining borrowing capacity. If the monetary figure represents the total size or limit of the credit facility, tag it with LineOfCreditFacilityMaximumBorrowingCapacity, regardless of surrounding availability language.
[err-00009] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not map interest charges on debt instruments (e.g., seller notes) to the generic tag InterestExpense; instead use the specific tag InterestExpenseDebt.

## CONTEXT CLUES & INDICATORS

[ctx-00004] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Look for keywords such as "mandatory convertible", "preferred stock", "convertible debt", or "conversion price". When a monetary figure is presented as the conversion price of such an instrument, map it to DebtInstrumentConvertibleConversionPrice1 instead of SaleOfStockPricePerShare.
[ctx-00006] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Keywords indicating a basis spread include "margin", "basis spread", "over LIBOR", "over the reference rate", or phrasing like "X% margin on the variable‑rate loan". When these appear, select DebtInstrumentBasisSpreadOnVariableRate1 instead of any interest‑rate‑related tag.
[ctx-00008] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When a figure (e.g., $75 million) is described as the size, limit, or total amount of a revolving credit facility, even if the sentence also mentions "availability" or "available to borrow," treat it as the facility's maximum borrowing capacity and use LineOfCreditFacilityMaximumBorrowingCapacity. Use LineOfCreditFacilityRemainingBorrowingCapacity only when the amount is explicitly stated as the portion still available for borrowing.
[ctx-00010] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Keywords such as "interest expense on seller notes", "interest charges on debt", "interest on debt instrument" indicate that the appropriate tag is InterestExpenseDebt rather than the generic InterestExpense.
## OTHERS
[misc-00002] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Tag selection rule: When a monetary figure refers to cash paid (or gross cash amount) to acquire a business, choose PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross. Reserve BusinessCombinationConsiderationTransferred1 for figures that represent total consideration transferred, which may include stock, earn‑outs, or other non‑cash elements.
