## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

[err-00001] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Avoid defaulting to broad tags like CommonStockSharesOutstanding when the text explicitly mentions shares issued as part of an acquisition; instead use BusinessAcquisitionEquityInterestsIssuedOrIssuableNumberOfSharesIssued.
[err-00002] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not map a purchase‑price amount (e.g., $162 million) to BusinessCombinationConsiderationTransferred1; such amounts represent gross payments to acquire a business and should be tagged PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross.
