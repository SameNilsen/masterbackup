| Feature | Typical Range |
|---------|---------------|
| **Length** | 80–100 ft (24–30 m) |
| **Weight** | 150–200 tons (≈ 140–180 000 kg) |

| **Lung capacity** | ~30 m³ (≈ 1 000 ft³) |

### Why it’s the biggest
- **Massive body size**: Blue whales dwarf all other mammals, including the gigantic land mammals like the African elephant.  
- **Massive organ sizes**: Their heart, lungs, and even their tongues (up to 9 kg) are proportionally enormous.  
- **Specialized physiology**: Their bodies are adapted to a fully aquatic lifestyle, allowing them to grow so large without the constraints of gravity that limit terrestrial animals.

### Quick comparison
- **Largest land mammal**: African elephant (*Loxodonta africana*) – up to ~12 m tall at the shoulder, ~6–7 tons.  
- **Largest marine mammal**: Blue whale – up to ~30 m long, ~200 tons.  

So, the blue whale holds the title of the largest mammal in the world.
