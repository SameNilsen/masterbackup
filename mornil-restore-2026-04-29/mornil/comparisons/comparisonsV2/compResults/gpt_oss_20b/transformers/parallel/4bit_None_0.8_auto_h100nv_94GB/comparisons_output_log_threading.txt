## How to carve your own meaning

1. **Reflect** – Ask yourself: What excites you? What pain points do you want to solve? What values feel non‑negotiable?
2. **Experiment** – Try new hobbies, volunteer, or take on a short-term challenge. Notice what evokes a sense of “flow.”
3. **Reframe setbacks** – View obstacles as opportunities for growth or for refining your purpose.
4. **Connect intentionally** – Invest in relationships that feed, challenge, and inspire you.
5. **Iterate** – Your meaning can evolve. Revisit your priorities every few years (or whenever a big life event happens).

## A small philosophical digression

> *“The meaning of life is to give it meaning.”* – **Soren Kierkegaard**  
> This is not a nihilistic shrug but a call to take ownership. Like a blank page, life offers infinite possibilities—your choices fill it.

## Final thought

Think of life as a project rather than a destination. The meaning is not a fixed endpoint but the sum of projects you’re **passionately working on**. It changes with age, experiences, and insight. Rather than hunting for a “one‑size‑fits‑all” answer, you’re better off building a personal narrative that feels authentic and fulfilling.  

> **Tip:** Write a one‑sentence “mission statement.” Refine it regularly. It anchors you and clarifies what truly matters.
